libfprint-2-tod-1-broadcom.PATCHED.so is a PATCHED copy of Dell/Canonical's
proprietary driver of the same name (obtained from Canonical's OEM repo:
https://git.launchpad.net/~oem-solutions-engineers/libfprint-2-tod1-broadcom ).

It carries NO open-source license and is NOT committed to this repository:
../build_from_upstream.sh fetches the stock binary and generates it locally.

The 9 byte-level modifications vs the stock binary are documented in ../PATCHES.md
and reproducible with ../patch_driver.py (verified byte-for-byte identical).
Check an installed driver with:  python3 ../patch_driver.py --check <driver.so>
